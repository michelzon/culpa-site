CulpaPrivacy policy

Request deletion of your data

This page applies to the Android game Culpa (package com.michelzondag.culpa) by developer Michel Zondag.

How to request deletion

  1. Email info@michelzondag.com with the subject “Culpa data deletion request”.
  2. Mention roughly when you played, and if you know them, your device’s advertising ID (Android Settings › Google › Ads) and/or a Google Play purchase transaction ID. Culpa has no accounts, so these are the only identifiers we can match.
  3. We confirm within 30 days once the data has been deleted from our systems and our processors (analytics, crash reporting, advertising attribution).

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted on request: analytics events, crash records and advertising-attribution records linked to your device or advertising identifiers.
  • On your device: your progress, settings and purchase state live only on your phone; uninstalling Culpa or clearing its storage deletes them immediately.
  • Kept for a limited time: Google Play purchase records that we must retain for accounting and refund handling (up to 7 years under Dutch law); aggregated statistics that cannot identify you.

Full details are in the privacy policy (English) or privacybeleid (Nederlands).
