Privacy Policy - Culpa

Last updated: 17 September 2026

This privacy policy explains what data the Culpa app ("Culpa", "the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android app published under the package com.michelzondag.culpa and, once released, the iOS version.

Culpa is developed by an individual developer, Michel Zondag ("the developer"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact info@michelzondag.com.

1. No account required

Culpa does not require you to create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, or any other directly identifying information to play the game. Your progress, purchases, and settings are stored on your device. There is no Google Play Games Services sign-in and no cloud save in this release: nothing carries your progress off the device.

2. What we collect, and why

Category Examples Why we collect it
Device and advertising identifiers Advertising ID (AAID), device model, OS version, IP-derived approximate location, app instance ID To show and measure ads, to detect fraud, and to understand which install came from which ad campaign
Purchase history Which in-app products you bought (e.g. remove_ads, case packs, hints), transaction ID, price and currency To unlock the content you paid for, to verify purchases, and to measure revenue by acquisition source
App interaction data Screens visited, cases started/solved/abandoned, hints used, ad views, session length, daily streak To understand how the app is used, fix problems, and calibrate puzzle difficulty
Crash and diagnostic data Stack traces, device state at time of crash, app version To find and fix bugs and stability issues

We do not collect your name, email address, precise GPS location, contacts, photos, or any content you did not choose to share (such as a shared solve time, which contains only a number and, if you opt in, your country).

3. Who processes this data

Culpa uses the following third-party services, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase Analytics - app usage and event data, to understand engagement and retention. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics - crash and diagnostic data, to keep the app stable.
  • Google AdMob, including its mediation partners - shows ads (rewarded video, interstitial, and optionally banner) and uses device/advertising identifiers to select and measure ads. Mediation partners may include other ad networks certified by Google; each operates under its own privacy policy, listed in the in-app UMP consent message.
  • Google Play Billing - processes your in-app purchases. Culpa never sees your payment card details; Google Play handles payment directly.
  • Google Cloud Firestore and Firebase Authentication - stores the world leaderboard entries described in §2 and hands out the anonymous player id they are filed under. No sign-in, name or email address is involved.

Not in this release: Culpa ships no Meta (Facebook) SDK and no Google Play Games Services. If either is ever added, this section and the Play Data safety form are updated before that build goes out.

We do not sell your data. Data is shared with these providers only to operate the app, show ads, process payments, and measure advertising performance.

4. Legal basis for processing

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK, and other regions with similar consent requirements: personalized advertising and related analytics/attribution processing occur only after you give consent through the in-app Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog, shown on first launch. If you decline or only partially consent, Culpa shows non-personalized ads and limits data collection accordingly, following the choices UMP reports back to the app (including any "under the age of consent" flag).
  • Outside the EEA/UK, where local law permits: we rely on legitimate interest to process device identifiers and interaction data for analytics, fraud prevention, and advertising, balanced against your rights. Where local law requires consent (e.g. under similar frameworks elsewhere), we obtain it through the same UMP flow, region-adjusted.
  • Purchase data is processed because it is necessary to perform our contract with you (delivering what you bought) and to comply with tax and accounting obligations.

5. Data retention

  • Analytics and crash data: retained by Google Firebase for up to 14 months (Firebase Analytics default) unless you request deletion sooner.
  • Purchase records: retained as long as needed to honor your purchase and for the legally required accounting period, typically up to 7 years.
  • Local app data (progress, settings, cached packs): stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Daily-case leaderboard entries (solve time and country only, no name unless you opt in): retained for a limited period for the leaderboard to function, then aggregated or deleted.

6. Children

Culpa is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Culpa is rated PEGI 7. Because the app is for users of 13 and up, we do not tag consent requests as coming from a user under the age of consent (tagForUnderAgeOfConsent is set to false), so everyone is asked the same consent question and everyone can reopen their choice from Settings. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us at info@michelzondag.com and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing already carried out)
  • Data portability, where applicable
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email info@michelzondag.com with your request. Because Culpa does not require an account, we may ask for details such as your device's advertising ID or purchase transaction ID to locate relevant data - we will never ask for your password or payment details.

8. Withdrawing advertising consent

You can review or change your advertising consent at any time in the app: Settings › Ad settings, which re-opens the UMP consent dialog. You can also limit ad tracking through your device's own settings (Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads, "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of ads personalization").

9. Data deletion requests

Because Culpa has no account system, most of your data lives only on your device and is deleted the moment you uninstall the app or clear its storage. To request deletion of data held by us or our processors (e.g. analytics or crash records tied to your device/advertising identifiers, or purchase records we must reconcile), email info@michelzondag.com with:

  1. The approximate date(s) you used the app
  2. Your device's advertising ID, if known (Settings › Privacy › Ads on Android), or a purchase transaction ID

We will confirm deletion within 30 days, except where we are legally required to retain purchase/accounting records for longer.

10. Security

Data in transit to our third-party processors (Firebase, AdMob, Google Play Billing) is encrypted using industry-standard TLS. We do not store payment card data ourselves; Google Play Billing handles that directly.

11. International data transfers

Our processors (all of them Google) may process and store data outside your country, including in the United States, under their respective standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards. See each provider's privacy policy linked in §3 for details.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes or as legal requirements evolve. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected in the in-app UMP consent flow where required.
